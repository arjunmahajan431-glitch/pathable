## 6. Limitations

- One city per report and a finite random sample: these are descriptive statistics, not a benchmark of every pedestrian network.
- The shortest route is length-only by definition. It is not a recommendation and may cross steps or restricted access.
- DEM-estimated slope is coarse (SRTM 30 m). It is not a sidewalk measurement and can be wrong at bridges, underpasses and building edges.
- Coverage counts mapping completeness, not accessibility, maintenance or safety.
- Runtime was measured on this machine in one process; it will differ elsewhere.
